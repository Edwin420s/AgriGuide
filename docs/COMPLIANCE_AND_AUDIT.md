# Regulatory Compliance and Audit Specification

## Audit Principles
AgriGuide is designed to meet emerging international standards for transparent, auditable artificial intelligence in food systems:

1. EU Artificial Intelligence Act (Risk Classification):
   - When AI directly controls critical physical infrastructure (e.g., automated irrigation pump stations and reservoir valves), it falls under high-scrutiny categories requiring logging, human oversight, and verifiable derivation proofs.
   - AgriGuide satisfies this by enforcing hard deterministic MeTTa safety clamps and generating immutable SHA-256 Replay Certificates for every recommendation.

2. ESG (Environmental, Social, and Governance) Reporting:
   - Scope 1 and Scope 2 Agricultural Emissions: Over-irrigation increases diesel pump generator runtime and grid electrical draw. AgriGuide logs cumulative water saved (cubic meters) and pump run-time avoided.
   - Nitrate Leaching Prevention: Fertilizer application pauses prior to heavy precipitation events prevent chemical runoff into regional watersheds.

3. Parametric Micro-Insurance Traceability:
   - AgriGuide logs chronological field state histories that settle drought and crop-failure claims without costly manual in-person field inspections.
