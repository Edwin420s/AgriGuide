## 3. Sensory Agronomic Descriptors

- **Kunyauka (Wilting)**: Used by farmers to describe midday moisture stress where leaves curl or drop turgor.
- **Kutuama kwa Maji (Waterlogging)**: Indicates poor drainage or excessive irrigation resulting in anoxic root conditions.
- **Ukame (Drought)**: Indicates extended period without effective precipitation.
- **Mbolea ya Kukuzia (Top-dressing Fertilizer)**: Nitrogen application (CAN / Urea) during rapid vegetative elongation.
