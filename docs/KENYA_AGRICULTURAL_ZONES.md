# Kenyan Agro-Ecological Zones and Farming Profiles

AgriGuide includes tailored agronomic parameters for Kenya's diverse agricultural micro-climates:

## 1. Central Highlands & Mt. Kenya (Kirinyaga, Nyeri, Embu, Meru)
- Elevation: 1,400m - 2,200m ASL
- Predominant Soils: Nitisols and volcanic loams with high water retention capacity.
- Primary Crops: Maize (grain filling), French beans (export horticulture), tea, coffee.
- Irrigation Profile: Furrow and micro-sprinkler drawing from Mt. Kenya glacial tributaries.

## 2. North Rift & Western Grain Basket (Uasin Gishu, Trans Nzoia, Bungoma)
- Elevation: 1,800m - 2,400m ASL
- Predominant Soils: Ferralsols and humic nitisols.
- Primary Crops: Large-scale commercial maize, wheat, canola.
- Irrigation Profile: Supplementary center-pivot and borehole abstraction during dry spells.

## 3. Eastern Semi-Arid Lowlands (Machakos, Makueni, Kitui)
- Elevation: 600m - 1,200m ASL
- Predominant Soils: Luvisols and sandy clay loams prone to rapid crusting.
- Primary Crops: Drought-tolerant sorghum, pearl millet, pigeon peas, green grams.
- Irrigation Profile: Strict drip conservation with farm pond rainwater harvesting.

## 4. Coastal Agro-Zone (Kilifi, Kwale, Taita Taveta)
- Elevation: 0m - 500m ASL
- Predominant Soils: Arenosols (rapid-draining sandy loam).
- Primary Crops: Cassava, cashew nut, coconut, organic chili.
- Irrigation Profile: High vapor pressure deficit (VPD) requiring frequent micro-dosing.
